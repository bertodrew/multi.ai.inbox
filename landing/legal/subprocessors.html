No analytics service, no third-party CDN and no advertising tracker is used: the site sets no cookie of its own. The server's egress IP is read from a public service (api.ipify.org) to build SPF records; that is an IP of the server, not of a person.

Changes

A new sub-processor is added to this page before it processes any customer data. Customers receive an email at the organization's administrative address at least 30 days in advance, and may object on data protection grounds; if an objection cannot be resolved, the affected service can be terminated without penalty. Questions and objections: [legal email].

Where the data lives

Mailboxes, the database and backups stay on servers in the European Union. There is no multi-region replication. Backups are encrypted on the server before they are uploaded, so the storage provider holds ciphertext; the vault key is held separately by the operator.
