3. Cookies and local storage

This website and the API set no cookie of their own and use no localStorage, no analytics, no advertising or profiling tracker, and no third-party CDN — therefore no consent banner is required. The only cookie you may receive comes from the webmail application (SOGo), which issues its own session cookie when you sign in; it is strictly necessary to keep the session alive and disappears when you sign out or the session expires.

7. Security

TLS on all surfaces; API credentials stored server-side encrypted with AES-256-GCM, never in logs, pages or the repository; container isolation; rate limiting; secret-free audit logs; encrypted backups on a dedicated volume; personnel access to mailbox contents only on the customer's authorized request, logged. On a breach with risk to data subjects: notification to the supervisory authority without undue delay (Art. 33) and to data subjects when high risk (Art. 34); for tenant mailboxes, immediate notification to the customer-controller per the DPA.

8. AI agents

Today no AI agent reads mailboxes (available tools operate at domain level). If activated: minimal per-task extraction, no model training on contents, actions towards third parties with human review, no exclusively automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects (Art. 22 GDPR), auditable per-agent keys, and an Art. 14 notice to correspondents before activation.

9. Your rights

Artt. 15–22 GDPR: access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection (Art. 21 at any time for legitimate-interest processing). Exercise: [privacy email] — response within 1 month. External senders/recipients: contact [privacy email]; for tenant mailboxes we are the processor and will assist the customer-controller (Art. 28(3)(e)). Complaint: Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (www.garanteprivacy.it), or the FDPIC in Switzerland, and in any case the controller.

RightHow it works today
Access and portability (Art. 15, 20) Self-service: GET /v0/export with a key holding data_export returns the organization's settings, inboxes, domains, webhooks, key metadata and the full audit log as one JSON file. Mailbox content is exported in full over IMAP, or message by message with the raw endpoint.
Erasure (Art. 17) Self-service: DELETE /v0/organization with the exact organization name as confirmation (permission data_delete, never implied by a full-access key). The response contains the export taken immediately before deletion. Database rows go at once, stored blobs within minutes; addresses stay quarantined for 90 days.
Rectification, restriction, objection Write to [privacy email]; we answer within one month, or explain why an extension applies.
No automated decision Account suspension or deletion is never decided by an algorithm alone; agent actions towards third parties require human review.

Requests that reach us and concern a customer's mailbox are forwarded to that customer, who is the controller: we assist them but do not answer on their behalf.
