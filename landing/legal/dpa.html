Data Processing Agreement

Art. 28 GDPR processing terms for tenant mailboxes. Version 1.0-draft, updated 2026-10-23.

DRAFT — structure and security annex are published for transparency and procurement; the binding text is the signed agreement. Fields marked [ ] are completed per customer, and the transfer impact assessment is still being prepared.

1. Roles and scope

For the mailboxes, email content and attachments of the Customer's users and of their correspondents, the Customer is the controller and AImulti.inbox is the processor. For contact, account and billing data of the Customer, AImulti.inbox is an independent controller (see the Privacy Policy). Processing covers hosting of mailboxes, reception, storage and transmission of email and attachments, inbound screening, attachment scanning, webhook delivery, security logging and support.

2. Processor obligations

  • Documented instructions only: the Terms, this agreement and the settings the Customer applies in the Service (for example screen_before_notify).
  • Confidentiality: access to mailbox content by personnel only on the Customer's authorised request, and logged.
  • Security: the measures in the annex below.
  • Sub-processors: only those on the published list, bound by the same obligations; changes announced 30 days in advance, with a right to object.
  • Data subject requests: self-service export and deletion in the API; a request that reaches us and concerns Customer data is forwarded without undue delay, not answered on the Customer's behalf.
  • Breach: notification to the Customer without undue delay after we become aware, with the information available under Art. 33(3).
  • Audit: information and cooperation for audits, at most once a year, 30 days notice, without access to other tenants' data.
  • Deletion or return at termination, with export available before deletion.

4. Transfers outside the EEA

Mailboxes and backups are hosted in the European Union (Hetzner Online GmbH, Germany). Transfers outside the EEA happen only towards the sub-processors marked as such on the sub-processor list, under the Standard Contractual Clauses (Decision (EU) 2021/914, module two) and a documented transfer impact assessment. 5. Deletion, backups and quarantine

At termination the Customer can export everything and then delete the organization: database rows go immediately, message blobs within minutes through a deletion queue, and deleted addresses stay quarantined for 90 days so they cannot be registered by someone else. Database dumps in the encrypted backup bucket expire by rotation (45-day lifecycle, 30-day immutable lock); immutable blob copies are removed through a dedicated procedure with the vault key.

6. Getting the signed agreement

Customers who need a countersigned DPA with their legal name, address and signature receive it before production use: write to [legal email]. The controller–processor split, the liability clauses and the transfer assessment are completed with qualified counsel and prevail over the Terms in case of conflict.
