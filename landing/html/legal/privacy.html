7. Security

TLS on all surfaces; API credentials stored server-side encrypted with AES-256-GCM, never in logs, pages or the repository; container isolation; rate limiting; secret-free audit logs; encrypted backups on a dedicated volume; personnel access to mailbox contents only on the customer's authorized request, logged. On a breach with risk to data subjects: notification to the supervisory authority without undue delay (Art. 33) and to data subjects when high risk (Art. 34); for tenant mailboxes, immediate notification to the customer-controller per the DPA.

8. AI agents

Today no AI agent reads mailboxes (available tools operate at domain level). If activated: minimal per-task extraction, no model training on contents, actions towards third parties with human review, no exclusively automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects (Art. 22 GDPR), auditable per-agent keys, and an Art. 14 notice to correspondents before activation.
