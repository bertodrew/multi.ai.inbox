5. Acceptable use policy (AUP)

Prohibited: sending unsolicited commercial email (UCE/UBE, "spam") or mail bombing; phishing; distributing malware; content illegal under Italian and European law; infringement of third-party rights; reverse engineering of APIs; deliberate overload of systems and APIs; address harvesting. The AUP is aligned with registrar policies (Namecheap Universal ToS §7, Hosting AUP §9).

Documented opt-in is required for bulk mail, with compliance to the Italian Privacy Code, GDPR and (where applicable) CAN-SPAM. Proof of consents may be requested.

Consequences. Spam or abuse by a single tenant on the shared IP can blacklist the IP and destroy deliverability for all hosted domains. Therefore:

  • immediate suspension of the responsible mailboxes, even without notice while the abuse is ongoing;
  • pass-through charge to the responsible Customer of third-party penalties — including the penalty of up to USD 500 that the registrar (Namecheap Hosting AUP §9) assesses in spam/blacklisting cases — plus documented delisting and mitigation costs;
  • reporting of manifestly unlawful uses to the authorities.

The Provider monitors outbound traffic (rspamd) and may impose per-mailbox sending limits to protect the shared reputation.
