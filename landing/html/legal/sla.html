Service Level Agreement

99.5% monthly uptime — measured, with service credits. Version 1.0-draft, updated 2026-10-19. Part of the Terms of Service.

DRAFT — pending review by qualified legal counsel (IT/CH). Credit tiers and response times are market proposals to be validated before publication.

1. Availability commitment

The Provider commits to a monthly uptime of 99.5% for mail (SMTP 25/465/587, IMAP 143/993) and webmail (HTTPS). Over a 30-day month, 99.5% equals at most ~216 minutes (3h 36m) of attributable downtime.

The service runs on a single instance (single-tenant, single-instance) on Hetzner (DE) without geographic redundancy. 99.5% reflects this architecture: higher figures (e.g. 99.9%) are not promised until redundancy/DR is implemented.

2. Measurement

Monthly uptime = (total minutes in the month − attributable downtime minutes) / total minutes, computed at month-end on CEST. Downtime is continuous or repeated unavailability of the services in §1, ascertained by the Provider's monitoring or reported by the Customer and verified. A monthly availability report is provided on request. [Monitoring system — to be defined.]

3. Exclusions

The following do not count against uptime and grant no credits:

  • scheduled maintenance notified ≥ 48h in advance, max 4h/month, in the 02:00–06:00 CEST window;
  • force majeure;
  • failures of the infrastructure provider (Hetzner) and its services (network, storage, power);
  • third-party outages not controlled by the Provider: connectors/registrar (Namecheap API), customer DNS managed externally, LLM providers, external public blacklists;
  • IP blacklisting caused by the Customer's content or practices (AUP violation);
  • suspension for AUP violation or non-payment;
  • customer-side issues (devices, connectivity, self-made misconfigurations);
  • services out of scope — e.g. inbox placement at third parties (deliverability disclaimer in the Terms §11).

4. Service credits

If monthly uptime falls below 99.5%, the Customer is entitled to a credit on the next month's fee, to be claimed within [30 days] of the monthly report:

Monthly uptimeCredit on the monthly fee
< 99.5% – ≥ 99.0%10%
< 99.0% – ≥ 98.0%25%
< 98.0% – ≥ 95.0%50%
< 95.0%100%

The credit is the sole remedy for missed uptime (except intent/gross negligence), does not cover indirect damages, and never exceeds the monthly fee. No credits are due where the suspension results from the Customer's violation.
