Environment

shared or dedicated

Your inboxes run in a shared environment on the multi-tenant cluster. A dedicated environment — your own isolated instance with its own IP and mail reputation — can be switched on at any time, in either direction.

Shared is billed by active mailboxes; dedicated as one flat plan per instance. Compare the two on pricing.
