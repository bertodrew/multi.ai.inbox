Pricing

Pick a shared or dedicated environment at signup — switch at any time, in either direction. Packages scale by mailboxes, domains and users. Enterprise is contact-sales only.

Pre-launch: prices are indicative list prices and are finalised at launch. All figures exclude VAT.

Choose your environment

Shared environment

Your inboxes run on a multi-tenant cluster in EU datacenters. Cheapest entry, billed by active mailboxes — the platform handles capacity for you.

Cost basis: Hetzner CX-class shared nodes (8 vCPU · 16 GB) at €15.99/mo + 5 TB Storage Box at €10.90/mo — Hetzner list prices, October 2026, excl. VAT.

Dedicated environment

Your own isolated instance: dedicated vCPU and RAM, your own IP, your own TLS and DKIM reputation. No neighbours, full control, billed as one flat plan.

Cost basis: Hetzner CCX-class dedicated-vCPU instances from €45/mo or AX-class root servers from €57.30/mo — list prices, October 2026, excl. VAT.

Billing per tenant

Every tier

Billing is per tenant, not per seat: one plan covers the tenant’s domains, mailboxes and agents. Metering counts active mailboxes, dunning is automated and payment rails are EU/CH. A DPA (Art. 28) is ready before the first invoice.

  • UnitPer tenant (shared) or per instance (dedicated)
  • MeteredActive mailboxes
  • CadenceMonthly
  • Payment railsEU/CH
  • SwitchingShared ↔ dedicated at any time, no lock-in
  • DPAReady before the first invoice
