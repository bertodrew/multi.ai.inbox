Pricing

Three service tiers for tenants of the multi-brand inbox platform. Every plan is per tenant and carries the same core: branding per brand, per-tenant billing and sovereign EU/CH inference.

Pre-launch: plans are qualitative until launch — Pricing finalised at launch. The comparison below shows what each tier covers, without prices.

One brand

For a single brand that wants its mail back under its own control: one domain, a small team and one agent, on shared EU/CH infrastructure.

Billing per tenant

Every tier

Billing is per tenant, not per seat: one plan covers the tenant’s domains, mailboxes and agents. Metering counts active mailboxes, dunning is automated and payment rails are EU/CH. A DPA (Art. 28) is ready before the first invoice.

  • UnitPer tenant
  • MeteredActive mailboxes
  • CadenceMonthly
  • Payment railsEU/CH
  • DPAReady before the first invoice
